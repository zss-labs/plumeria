## 22.3.4 and later
The later attribute wins:  

Starting in 22.3.4, a `className` or `style` attribute written after `sx` overrides the generated one instead of being merged into it. `<div sx={styles.text} className="banner" />` renders only `banner`. When `sx` comes after, as in `<div className="banner" sx={styles.text} />`, the generated `className` wins and `banner` is dropped. The same applies to a `className` or `style` passed to a styled component. To override a style, use `sx`.

## margin-trim

`marginTrim` accepts `none`, `block`, `block-start` and `block-end`. `in-flow` and `all` were removed from the spec, and the ESLint plugin now reports them.
